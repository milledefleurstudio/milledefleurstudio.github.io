The Garden

A garden beside the pines,
a wedding at ease for everyone

Mille de fleur is an outdoor wedding venue beside a pine forest in Yuseong, Daejeon. In spring and autumn, we host just one wedding a day in our 6,600 m² garden. We do more than offer a space: from the entrance to the last farewell, we shape the day with you so that both families can feel at ease.

The Walk
45m

From the very first step

Forty-five meters lead from the house to the garden. Some fathers meet the bride halfway; some couples walk in together. The anticipation along the way becomes the opening scene of every wedding here.

Flowers

A garden completed by flowers

Our founder, a florist and creative director, directs the flowers for every wedding. Using only fresh flowers, we create a mood that belongs to the two of you.

Ceremony

Daytime Wedding

Sat 12:00 · Sun 12:00

A ceremony on the lawn, in natural light falling through the pines. An after-party is available for daytime weddings, too.

Evening Party Wedding

Sat 17:00

A party-style wedding, spending the evening with those closest to you. For 40 to 72 guests. Venue hire 16:00–18:30.

On a Rainy Day

Rain or shine

If it rains, the wedding goes on under our large tent and indoors. We have shared many days made more special by the rain.
